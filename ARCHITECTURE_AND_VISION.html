01 · The Problem We Kill

Four silent ways agencies lose money

Every capability in CarHub exists to close one of these four leaks.

⚠️
Compliance Risk

A car rented with expired insurance is an uninsured liability. Agencies discover expiries after the incident.

🛡️
The Guardian

Vehicles with expired mandatory documents are hard-locked from booking — the API physically refuses the rental.

👻
Invisible Demand

Every customer turned away — "no car available" — is unrecorded, unrecoverable revenue.

📈
Refusal Log & Waitlist

Every refusal is captured with its potential revenue in dirhams, queued for recovery when a vehicle frees up.

🌫️
Cash-Flow Blindness

Quoted prices get mistaken for collected cash. Owners find the gap at month-end — or never.

✅
Verified-Payment Accounting

Every metric on the platform is computed from verified cash stamped by a manager — never from quotes.

🕳️
Zero Accountability

Prices change, bookings vanish — and nobody knows who did it, when, or why.

🔍
Forensic Audit Trail

Every mutation records the actor, the before-state and the after-state as JSON snapshots, plus IP and timestamp.

The tenant boundary, end to end

JWT { sub · role · agencyId } → @CurrentUser() → service(agencyId, …) → WHERE agencyId = ?

Tenancy is enforced in the service layer on every query — never trusted to the client. Composite uniques like (agencyId, licensePlate) isolate agencies while allowing global reuse.

03 · One Booking, End to End

Where the Guardian earns its name

A single reservation request triggers compliance enforcement, demand capture, and triple-linked accounting — automatically.

sequenceDiagram
    participant O as Owner (Browser)
    participant N as Next.js UI
    participant A as NestJS API
    participant P as Prisma / DB
    O->>N: "Execute Reservation"
    N->>A: POST /api/bookings (JWT cookie)
    A->>A: AuthGuard → RolesGuard → DTO validation
    A->>P: Guardian check — expired mandatory docs?
    alt Vehicle hard-locked
        A->>P: Auto-log LOST refusal
        A-->>N: 403 "Vehicle Hard-Locked: INSURANCE expired"
    else Vehicle compliant
        A->>P: Overlap check — dates collide?
        alt Dates collide
            A->>P: Auto-log WAITING refusal + potential revenue
            A-->>N: 400 "Added to waitlist"
        else Clear
            A->>P: TRANSACTION — Booking + Invoice (20% VAT) + PENDING Payment
            A->>P: Forensic audit entry (actor, before, after)
            A->>A: Fire BOOKING_CREATED webhooks
            A-->>N: 201 Booking
        end
    end
    N-->>O: Toast + live dashboard refresh
The "triple link": no booking exists without its invoice and its pending payment — created atomically

04 · Core Business Domains

Six pillars, one console

🧠

Command Centre — Analytics

Verified revenue MTD with growth, 30-day fleet utilization, Guardian block queue, recoverable-demand value, 14-day intensity chart, star-asset ROI ranking, and a humanized live audit feed — one glance, five answers.

🚘

Fleet & The Guardian

Every vehicle carries live document countdowns, mechanical history, odometer sync, and a continuously recomputed ROI score. Expired mandatory documents hard-lock the asset from booking — renewals restore it in one click.

👥

CRM & Bookings

Identity-first rentals (CIN/passport required, walk-in quick-register), a Gantt-style visual scheduler with a live "now" line, a server-enforced booking state machine, blacklisting that preserves history, and Puppeteer-rendered PDF contracts.

💰

Financial Vault

Booking → Invoice (20% VAT) → PENDING payment, atomically. Cash counts only when a manager verifies it — the verifier's identity is stamped forever. Expenses, deposits in escrow states, P&L pulse and CSV export.

🕸️

The Hub — B2B Fleet Sharing

Idle vehicles get listed at wholesale B2B rates; agencies with excess demand rent from each other. Every new agency deepens inventory liquidity — the network-effect moat.

🔐

Security & Forensic Auditing

Helmet headers, rate limiting, HttpOnly JWT, DTO whitelisting, role guards, tenant-scoped queries — and an AuditLog capturing actor + before/after JSON + IP for every mutation on the platform.

07 · Flagship Upgrade

Global Client Reputation & Blacklist Network

Today each agency's blacklist is an island: a renter who damages a car in Casablanca rents again in Marrakech tomorrow. The Reputation Network turns tenant-isolated data into an industry-wide risk shield — without breaking tenant privacy.
