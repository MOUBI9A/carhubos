# Fider
**Type:** service
**Summary:** Public brand for CarHub OS, a multi-tenant operating system and trust layer for Moroccan car-rental agencies.
## Key Facts
- Platform role: `ROOT_ADMIN`; agency roles: `OWNER`, `MANAGER`, `AGENT`; renter role: `RENTER`.
- Money is MAD with 20% VAT inclusive and Prisma Decimal arithmetic.
- Production uses Vercel multi-service routing for Next.js and the Nest backend container.
